## Simple brand rationale

**lilimio.** connects *lilim*, the Filipino word for shade, with a short, modern app-style ending. It suits an app that helps people understand the heat, plan outdoor time, and find relief: a digital companion for finding a little shade in everyday life.

The **-io** ending gives the name a contemporary technology identity. This is a proposed brand explanation, not a claim that -io is a Filipino word or that an official acronym was supplied. The small final dot belongs to the visual wordmark.

The base word is documented in [Diksiyonaryo.ph: lilim](https://diksiyonaryo.ph/search/lilim), which describes shelter/shade created by something blocking light from above, commonly a tree.
