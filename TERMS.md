## Understand the heat guidance

Forecasts are model estimates, not on-site measurements or official PAGASA observations. Calculated heat index describes shade and light-wind conditions; direct sun, exertion, clothing and individual circumstances can change risk. The experimental activity planner compares forecast windows. It is not a medically validated safe-exposure limit, a diagnosis, or a guarantee that an activity is safe.

Use current conditions, your own needs, and official local guidance when deciding what to do. Seek professional or emergency help when needed; lilimio. is not an emergency service. Official-source links do not imply that a government agency endorses this app or that official alerts are automatically synchronized.

## Check places before travelling

Relief locations are community-mapped OpenStreetMap records, not an inspected or verified cooling-centre registry. Hours, public access, shade, air conditioning, water quality and accessibility may be missing, incorrect or change without notice. A mapped water point is not a current drinking-water safety inspection. Confirm access and conditions with the place or local authority. Do not enter restricted property.
